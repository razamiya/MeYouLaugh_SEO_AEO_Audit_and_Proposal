AI search (AEO) plan

More shoppers ask ChatGPT, AI Overviews, AI Mode and Gemini things like "where can I buy funny Christian t-shirts?". Today MeYouLaugh has zero mentions across all four. The site isn't blocking AI crawlers (AI Search Health 89%), so the job is to give them something worth citing.

llms.txt file

A plain summary of the store, main categories and key pages that AI tools can read. The audit flagged it as missing.

Answer-first content

Guides and FAQs open with a short, direct answer, then the detail. AI tools quote pages that answer clearly.

Full schema markup

Product, Organization, FAQ and Breadcrumb schema give AI systems structured facts about your brand.

Merchant Center free listings

Products in Google Shopping, Images and AI shopping results at no ad cost. One of the fastest wins for a small store.

Consistent brand profile

Same name, logo and description across the site, Facebook, Instagram, X and Pinterest.

Reviews and mentions

Genuine customer reviews and listings in gift guides and apparel roundups, which AI answers often draw from.
